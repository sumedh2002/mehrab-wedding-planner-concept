The Mehrab team

The rituals

An Indian wedding is six celebrations, not one. Choose a ritual to see what we take off your family’s hands.

Roka

An intimate family gathering to bless the match. We keep it warm, simple and easy to put together at short notice.

What we handle

  • Home or venue setup
  • Florals and puja arrangements
  • Catering and a photographer
  • Gifting and shagun trays

Mehendi

A long, happy afternoon of henna, folk music and swings, planned so nobody waits two hours for their turn.

What we handle

  • Mehendi artists sized to your guest list
  • Floral swings, seating and shade
  • Dhol, folk singers and games
  • Live chaat and chai counters

Haldi

The messiest, most joyful morning of the week. We make the mess beautiful, and make sure it stays where it should.

What we handle

  • Marigold décor and petal showers
  • Splash-proof seating and covers
  • Fresh haldi and ubtan
  • Breakfast and drinks stations

Sangeet

Family performances, a proper stage and a dance floor that stays full long after the last rehearsed number.

What we handle

  • Stage, sound and lighting
  • A choreographer for family acts
  • Show running order and an anchor
  • DJ and the after-party

Pheras

The heart of the wedding, timed to the muhurat to the minute, with every elder seated where they can see.

What we handle

  • Mandap design and build
  • Pandit ji and puja samagri
  • Baraat and varmala moments
  • A muhurat-perfect run of show

Reception

A polished evening for your wider circle: your first night as a couple, and the one everyone photographs.

What we handle

  • Stage and photo moments
  • Menu tastings and the bar
  • Seating plans and guest flow
  • The send-off
